About Sliding Windows by Deluxe

We are a New Milford window company built around one practical idea: the right style in the right opening. A sliding window may be ideal in one room and a casement better in the next.

That is why our range covers sliding, vinyl, aluminum, bay and casement windows, alongside new window installation for houses and for commercial spaces.

Our customers include homeowners updating tired windows, landlords refreshing several units, and businesses that need dependable glazing for offices and storefronts.

You can reach us Monday to Friday between 10 am and 6 pm and on Saturdays from 10 am to 3 pm. We are closed on Sundays.
